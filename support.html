Support

Need help? Email matthew@emjaehorizon.com. Include your device model, software version, puzzle number, and what happened.

How do I play?

Fill every square with a Solid or a Ring. The numbers at the edges tell you how many Solids belong in each row and column. The two squares with the same symbol need one Solid and one Ring between them. Tap How to play on the board to see the rules.

How do I get back to my puzzles?

Open Journey and tap Continue to resume. Open Chapters to find an available puzzle or replay one you completed.

What if my progress looks wrong after an update?

Keep the app installed and contact support. Tell us the last puzzle you remember completing and which puzzle you were working on. Deleting the app can remove your saved game.

Where are my purchased stamps?

Open Get stamps from Journey. If you see Retry stamp delivery, use it to finish an outstanding purchase. If stamps are still missing, contact support with the pack size and approximate purchase time. Keep Apple Account and payment details private.

How do I report an ad?

Tap Report an ad below the puzzle controls or in Journey. You can copy the report or open an email draft. You choose what to send.
