Puzzle to Places

Beta Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy covers the private iPhone beta of Puzzle to Places and information you choose to share with us about the game.

Your game on this iPhone

The app saves your completed puzzles, board placements, solve times, hint use, stamp balance, destination guesses, and settings on your device. You can play without creating an account. Deleting the app can remove your saved progress.

Optional playtest reports

Gameplay counts are off by default. If you turn them on, the app records progress checkpoints and counts of how you use hints, stamps, and ads. You can turn off Gameplay counts under Journey, then Playtest tools, to clear those counts and keep your puzzle progress.

If you enter a name in Playtest results, it stays on your device and appears when you copy that report. Copying a report puts it on your clipboard. You choose whether to share it.

Test ads and privacy choices

We use Google’s advertising and privacy tools to check consent and show demo ads. Google may receive information such as device identifiers, ads viewed or interacted with, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from your internet connection. When Google offers privacy settings for your region, you can review or change them under Ad privacy choices on Journey.

Support and beta feedback

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. An ad report can include the placement, an ad response reference, and your description. We use messages and reports to respond, investigate, and improve the game. We keep them as long as needed for that work or as required by law. For questions or to request access to or deletion of information you shared, email matthew@emjaehorizon.com.

Apple’s TestFlight service may share testing metrics, crash reports, and feedback you submit. Apple’s Privacy Policy
