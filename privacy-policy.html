Puzzle to Places

Privacy Policy

This policy covers Puzzle to Places on iPhone and iPad and information you choose to share with us about the game.

Your saved game

You can play without an account. Completed puzzles, board placements, solve times, hint use, stamp balance, destination guesses, and settings stay on your device. Deleting the app can remove your save.

Optional gameplay reports

Gameplay counts are off by default. If you turn them on, the app keeps progress checkpoints and counts of hints, restarts, and ads on your device. Turn off Gameplay counts under Journey, then Playtest tools, to clear those counts and keep your puzzle progress.

If you enter a name in Playtest results, it is saved on your device and included when you copy that report. Copying a report puts it on your clipboard. You choose whether and where to share it.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app requests non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from your connection, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and protect its ad services.

When Google offers regional privacy settings, you can open Ad privacy choices from Journey. Turning off Gameplay counts controls the optional local report. Find more information in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Stamp purchases

Apple processes payments. The app stores which stamp packs it has credited and your balance on your device. We do not receive your card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple’s handling of purchase information.

Support and feedback

If you contact us, we receive the contact information, message, and files you choose to send. An ad report may contain the placement and an ad response reference. We use support information to respond and investigate. We keep it for as long as needed for that work or as required by law.

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple may share testing metrics, diagnostics, and feedback with us under its privacy policy.

Visiting these pages

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the support page. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Contact

To ask about privacy or request access to or deletion of information you sent us, email matthew@emjaehorizon.com.
